## Abstract Submission for: 
### New Project / Project News
We cordially invite CENTRA researchers, practitioners, and professionals to submit brief abstracts for ongoing project updates and new project proposals. CENTRA 8 will feature a "warm-up" session, offering a valuable opportunity to present your ideas, receive feedback, and connect with potential collaborators.

### Technical Paper
Authors are encouraged to submit original research, case studies, and innovative solutions that address current challenges and advancements in ICT-related fields. Papers should offer valuable insights, practical applications, and contribute meaningfully to the ongoing discourse in the industry. All submissions will undergo a rigorous peer-review process, and selected papers will be invited to present at the CENTRA 8 Technical Presentation Session.

## Submission Guidelines:
- Authors are required to submit their abstracts via [Abstract Submission Form](https://forms.gle/9vXfEKD95WiBwQLv6) and adhere to the provided instructions.

## Important Dates:

- Paper submissions deadline: December 12, 2025
- Notification of acceptance: January 10, 2025
- Camera-ready deadline: February 9, 2025

For more information, contact the event organisers at [centra8.secretariat@gmail.com](mailto:centra8.secretariat@gmail.com).

## Registration
All Presentors must be registered for CENTRA 8. Details about registration and procedures can be found in the menu on the left. For any inquiries or further information, contact centra8.secretariat@gmail.com.

We look forward to an engaging session exploring the worldwide views of advanced ICT, highlighting cutting-edge innovations, emerging trends, and the global impact of technology on industries, societies, and future developments.
