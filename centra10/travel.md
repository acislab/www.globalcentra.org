## Travel Information

The recommended nearest airport is [**Portland International Airport (PDX)**](https://www.flypdx.com/).

### Ground Transportation from PDX to Corvallis

There is an hourly shuttle service available through [Groometransportation Oregon](https://groometransportation.com/oregon/) that you can book online to travel from PDX airport, with drop-offs at both the Courtyard Marriott in downtown Corvallis and the Hilton Garden Inn near the OSU campus. The shuttle commute takes approximately 2.5 hours and fares are currently $59-$69 for adults.

Once you arrive in PDX, locate the exit from Baggage Claim closest to carousel 10 out to the roadway. Use the crosswalk to access Island 2. Proceed to the Scheduled Airporter pickup location.

If you plan to rent a car, several rental car companies are available at PDX at [Portland Airport Rental Cars](https://www.flypdx.com/GroundTransportation/RentalCars); the drive to Corvallis is approximately 80 miles (130 km) and takes approximately 1 hour and 45 minutes, depending on traffic.

### Air Travel

Another nearby airport is **Eugene Airport (EUG)**. If you plan to fly to Eugene, the main transportation option is a rental car; the drive to Corvallis is approximately 35 miles (56 km) and takes approximately 50 minutes, depending on traffic.
