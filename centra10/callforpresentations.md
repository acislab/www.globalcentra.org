## Call for Submissions

The CENTRA 10 Technical Program Committee (TPC) welcomes submissions for the program. The submission deadline is October 30, 2026. There are different types of submissions; the following summary helps guide which type to choose. Please follow the respective link(s) to start your submission(s) in the Sched CFP site; if you have any questions, please email us at: centra_10@lists.engr.oregonstate.edu

If you are representing an [existing, ongoing CENTRA project](https://www.globalcentra.org/projects/) or [member institution](https://www.globalcentra.org/members/), you will have a guaranteed time slot in the CENTRA 10 program to present updates on behalf of your project or institution. In order for the organizers to properly plan the program, please [submit a short update (up to 800 words)](https://cfp.sched.com/speaker/7cpX4CTz0Z/event).

New to CENTRA? If you are a new or returning attendee and wish to present your work in one of the topics of interest below, you can choose from two options:

- If you are new or returning to CENTRA and wish to propose a new collaborative project, you may [submit a new project proposal (up to 800 words)](https://cfp.sched.com/speaker/sZrcydx6u9/event). Please check the list of [existing CENTRA projects](https://www.globalcentra.org/projects/) for examples and to check if one already closely matches your area of interest. If you submit a new project proposal, the expectation is that you will engage, organize, and lead discussions with other CENTRA partners during and after CENTRA 10; the program will include breakout time to talk with potential collaborators.

### Topics of Interest

- AI for Science
- Novel AI Architectures and Data Center Technologies
- Advanced Computing Cyberinfrastructure, including but not limited to software-defined, quantum, and HPC systems
- Agentic AI and Multi-Agent Systems
- Data Engineering and Analytics for AI
- Edge-Cloud Continuum Architectures
- Federated Learning and Distributed AI Frameworks
- Transdisciplinary Applications, including but not limited to smart agriculture, health, cities, and manufacturing
- Energy-Efficient Scalable Computing
