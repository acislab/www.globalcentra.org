We are pleased to welcome you to CENTRA 10 on behalf of the co-hosting institutions Oregon State University (OSU) and University of Oregon (UO). We look forward to your participation and contributions to transnational collaboration, and hope you will enjoy your stay in the beautiful Willamette Valley in the USA’s Pacific Northwest.
<br /><br />
CENTRA provides a unique platform for researchers, technologists, and policy-makers to collaboratively advance science and technology for the benefit of global and regional communities. This year’s theme, “AI-Enabled Transdisciplinary Science for Global Challenges​“ emphasizes collaborations across disciplinary boundaries of AI infrastructure systems and applications for societal impact. For the co-hosting institutions, this meeting marks an important step in fostering impactful collaborations poised to leverage OSU’s forthcoming Huang Collaborative Innovation Complex and UO’s recently established School of Computer and Data Sciences.
<br /><br />
Thank you for contributing to this global dialogue. We are excited to support you throughout the meeting and look forward to the collaborations that may begin here.
