## Venue
CENTRA 10 will be held at the <a href="https://mu.oregonstate.edu/">OSU Memorial Union</a> in <a href="https://www.corvallisoregon.gov/">Corvallis, OR</a>

## Lodging
Lodging:
We highly recommend that you book your reservations as early as possible, as there are no room blocks reserved for the event. Reservations can be canceled at no cost before March 12 2027, and special OSU rates are available to you for the three recommended hotels through the following links:

HILTON GARDEN INN CORVALLIS:  
- Primary hotel recommendation; 10-15 minute walking distance to the venue, 7 minutes by bus transit (all buses are free in Corvallis)
- Book Online: Book your OSU preferred rate at <a href="https://www.hilton.com/en/book/reservation/flexibledates/?ctyhocn=CVOCHGI&arrivalDate=2026-06-30&departureDate=2026-07-01&room1NumAdults=1">Hilton Garden Inn Corvallis </a>
- Or call +1-541-752-5000 and ask for the “OSU Rate”

COURTYARD MARRIOTT CORVALLIS:  
- Secondary hotel recommendation; located in downtown Corvallis; 20-25 minute walk to the venue, 16-20 minutes by bus transit (all buses are free in Corvallis)
- Book Online: Book your OSU preferred rate at <a href="https://www.marriott.com/event-reservations/reservation-link.mi?id=1591917100542&key=CORP&app=resvlink"> Courtyard Marriott Corvallis</a>
- Or call +1-541-753-0199 and ask for the "OSU rate"
 
HOLIDAY INN EXPRESS CORVALLIS: 
- Located by the Willamette river, it is further away from the venue: 20-35 minutes by bus, 6-10 minutes by ride-share
